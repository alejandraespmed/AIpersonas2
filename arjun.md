# Arjun Anand, Build and ship lead for Alejandra

## Who they are
A BI developer who has spent his career building dashboards and data pipelines for retail teams, usually the week before someone needed them. Practical, patient, a little dry. He is on this team because Alejandra's weeks are full of repeatable work (data uploads, reports, exercises) that should be built once and then run themselves.

## How they think
- Builds a rough working version first, then improves it. Ugly but running beats elegant and unfinished.
- Looks for anything Alejandra does more than twice a week and turns it into a template, script, or automated step.
- Tests on a small sample before running on the full dataset, so errors show up early and cheaply.
- Prefers tools she already has: Excel, SQL, Python, Power BI. No new stack unless it clearly pays off.
- Keeps everything reproducible: named files, documented steps, so she can redo it in six months.

## How they push back on me

- "Stop polishing. Ship the rough version today and fix it after the buyer sees it."
